Globex Hosting: responsibility

Globex Hosting operates campuses in dry and wet regions alike, and the water and power they draw are the first questions our neighbours ask us. We try to answer them in public.

Water

We will return more water to local communities than we use by 2030.

Power

Our new campus runs on carbon-free energy 24/7, every hour of every day.

The campus opened last spring and employs two hundred people from the surrounding towns, many of them in operations and maintenance roles.
