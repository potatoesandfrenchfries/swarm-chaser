Sustainability at Acme Cloud

Acme Cloud runs infrastructure for thousands of teams across three continents, and we think a lot about what that infrastructure costs the places it lives. This page explains how we measure it and what we have promised.

Energy

100% of the electricity our data centers consumed was matched with renewable energy in 2025, for the third year in a row.

We have achieved carbon-neutral operations at all of our owned sites.

Customer Beta said that moving to Acme Cloud halved their costs and was 100% painless.

Operations

Our newest regions use free-air cooling wherever the local climate allows it, which cuts the energy spent on cooling in those regions.
